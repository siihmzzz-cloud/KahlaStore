# KA7LA Store — Launch Film Plan

- **Project:** KA7LA Store, a considered everyday-fashion storefront.
- **Audience:** People browsing contemporary essentials for men, women, and kids.
- **What distinguishes it:** A spare editorial art direction, a large graphic brand hero, a seamlessly looping “Every fit tells a story” ticker, and a compact path from collection browsing to selecting a size.
- **Most impressive claim:** The editorial ticker moves continuously on its own, independent of scrolling.
- **Visual hook:** The KA7LA wordmark crosses the grayscale campaign photograph on warm paper, then resolves into real storefront pages.
- **Actual UI flow:** Home hero → Men/Women/Kids collections → continuous editorial ticker → best-of product cards → Studio Heavyweight Tee detail, size selection, and add-to-bag affordance → branded end card.
- **Tone:** Restrained, confident, tactile, editorial.
- **Length / format:** About 20 seconds, 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps; original understated electronic bed.
- **Share caption:** “Everyday essentials. Unmistakable style. Meet KA7LA Store.”
- **Disclosure / accuracy:** No sales, shipping, or payment claims. The film presents the real UI and sample catalog, and does not show admin, account, or customer data.
